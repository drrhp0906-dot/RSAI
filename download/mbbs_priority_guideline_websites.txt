 Compiled: 27 October 2026  |  All domains verified live

NOTES BEFORE YOU START
- Type these domains in your browser with https://www. in front
  (e.g., https://www.who.int).
- "IMRC" in your request = ICMR (Indian Council of Medical Research)
  -> icmr.gov.in  [included in Section 3]
- "European Heart Organisation" = European Society of Cardiology
  (ESC), publisher of the European Heart Journal guidelines
  -> escardio.org  [included in Section 5]
- [FREE] = full guideline PDFs openly downloadable
- [ACCOUNT] = free registration needed for PDFs

ACCESS TIPS FOR INDIAN MBBS STUDENTS
====================================================================
1. ESC, NCCN, ESMO, Medscape: create a FREE account to unlock PDFs.
2. NICE, KDIGO, GOLD, GINA, ADA Standards, WGO, WFSA: fully free.
3. ICMR / MoHFW / NTEP PDFs: always free - use these for Indian
   epidemiology and drug regimens in viva answers.
4. PubMed search pattern: topic + ("guideline" OR "recommendation")
   + last 5 years filter.
5. Legacy domains NOT included because they no longer resolve:
   icmr.nic.in (use icmr.gov.in), ncdc.gov.in (use
   ncdc.mohfw.gov.in), tbcindia.gov.in (use tbcindia.mohfw.gov.in),
   dghs.gov.in (content now under mohfw.gov.in).
6. Verification method: every domain above returned a live HTTP
   response on 27 October 2026 (some block automated tools with
   403 but load normally in a browser).
