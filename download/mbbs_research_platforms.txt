NOTES BEFORE YOU START
- This file covers the DAILY-USE platforms: databases, journals,
  clinical reference, drug resources, education & research tools.
  (Guideline bodies like WHO/ESC/AHA/ICMR are in your first file.)
- Type domains with https://www. in front where needed.
- [FREE] = fully free  |  [ACCOUNT] = free registration
- Some sites block automated checks with 403 but open normally in
  your browser (they are confirmed live).

====================================================================
ACCESS TIPS FOR INDIAN MBBS STUDENTS
====================================================================
1. Power combo: Zotero (citations) + Unpaywall (free PDFs) + PubMed
   filters (last 5 years, free full text) covers 90% of paper needs.
2. Medscape and MIMS India accounts are free - the interaction
   checker is gold for pharmacology vivas.
3. Check your college library for UpToDate / BMJ Best Practice
   institutional logins before paying for anything.
4. Merck Manuals, LITFL, TeachMeAnatomy, Zero To Finals, Ninja Nerd
   are 100% free - ideal when money is tight.
5. For any ICMR/Student research project: register on CTRI if it is
   a trial, get an ORCID early, and screen papers in Rayyan.
6. Excluded at compile time because unreachable: dams.co.in (DAMS),
   ctri.nic.in (legacy CTRI address - the registry lives at
   ctri.gov.in), journals.lww.com mirror of IJMR (use ijmr.org.in).
7. Verification: every listed domain returned a live HTTP response
   on 27 October 2026 (fda.gov and cdsco.gov.in confirmed via a
   separate network path - they block simple automated checks but
   open normally in browsers).
